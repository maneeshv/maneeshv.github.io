An eight-world running adventure

Coastline Rush

From a dusk harbor to the edge of a black hole. Run, jump, and roll across eight worlds, master their hazards, and keep your momentum through every portal.

A native game for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with campaign, endless, and practice modes. Gameplay works offline and does not require an account.

Game support

For help or feedback, email maneeshv2601@gmail.com. For a technical issue, include your device model, operating system version, and what happened.

Do not include passwords or payment information.

Your game, your choice

Progress and personal replay ghosts stay on your device. On iPhone and iPad, optional rewarded ads can double the coins from a finished run. The Mac version is ad-free.
