Coastline Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Publisher: Maneesh

Privacy contact: maneeshv2601@gmail.com

This policy explains how Coastline Rush handles information on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, and when you use its support website or contact Maneesh.

Playing and saving your game

You do not need an account to play. The game stores your progress, coins, unlocks, scores, challenges, settings, saved run, and personal replay ghosts on your device. These records let you resume play and keep your achievements and preferences. The game does not upload these records to a game server or provide an online leaderboard or its own cloud synchronization. Your device's backup service may include local app data according to your backup settings.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location. It does not include a separate analytics service operated by the publisher.

Optional advertising on iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad version can offer an optional rewarded ad after a run. Completing an eligible ad earns additional in-game coins; these coins have no cash value. You can skip the offer and keep the coins already earned through play. The native Mac version does not include advertising or Google's advertising and consent SDKs.

When advertising is enabled, Google's User Messaging Platform contacts Google at app launch to determine applicable privacy choices. Choosing to watch an ad can open a consent form before an ad is requested. Declining to watch ads does not prevent this initial consent-information request.

Ads use Google AdMob. Google and advertising providers may process network and device information, IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash and performance information. Purposes include delivering and measuring ads, operating consent choices, detecting abuse and fraud, and maintaining and improving services. Google's SDK data disclosure describes supported collection; actual processing depends on configuration and consent.

The game requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party advertising ID and location-permission-based coarse-location collection, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission to access the advertising identifier. These choices do not mean no information is processed: IP-derived approximate location and operational advertising data may still be used. Read how Google uses information from partner apps and Google's Privacy Policy for its practices, service providers, international processing, and safeguards.

Your choices

You can play without watching a rewarded ad. Where Google's consent system requires an ongoing choice, Ad privacy options appears in the game's Settings and on the results screen. Use it to review or change your choices. A change affects subsequent processing; it does not undo processing already performed.

On iPhone or iPad, deleting the app through system settings removes its local game data; offloading the app retains documents and data. Separately manage any device backups that contain copies. Local progress is needed to retain your game achievements, so removing it loses that progress. Removing local game data does not delete information previously processed by Google.

Retention and privacy requests

Local game data remains on the device until removed with the app's data. Google applies its own retention rules to information it processes; see Google's retention explanation. We do not maintain a server copy of your game progress and cannot restore or retrieve it from a game account.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information or withdraw consent. Contact maneeshv2601@gmail.com about the publisher's handling of information. Requests concerning information held by Google can also use the controls and contact routes in Google's Privacy Policy.

Support and this website

If you email us, we use the email address, message, and any attachments you provide to respond to your request. Support uses Gmail. Correspondence remains in the support mailbox until deleted; there is no automatic deletion schedule. You can ask us to delete your correspondence by emailing maneeshv2601@gmail.com. Google's storage and backup practices also apply to mail it processes. Please avoid sending passwords, financial details, or other information unnecessary for support.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, embedded external assets, or forms. GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses and technical logs, to provide and secure hosting. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service governed by its own privacy practices.

Children

Coastline Rush is a general-audience game and is not primarily directed at children. Parents or guardians with concerns about a child's information can contact maneeshv2601@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our information practices change and show the effective date above. Contact maneeshv2601@gmail.com with privacy questions.
